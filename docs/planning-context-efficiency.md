# Planning context efficiency

Implemented destination kinds (`asset`, `url`, `on_page_form`), stable source transport references derived from immutable chunk IDs, stage-specific evidence selection, scoped confirmation, and a concrete-gap research gate. Full originals and execution packages remain stored; source selection does not summarize passages. Citations use original chunk IDs; stable transport labels are not citation IDs. Hash-label collisions fail rather than aliasing unrelated sources.

Selection retains uncertain applicability, legal/strategic rules, explicit dependencies, prior conflict sources and confirmed authority counterparts. Conflict discovery conservatively filters explicit channel scope rather than deferring copy rules that could conflict with a brief. Planning can defer identifiable execution-only sections. Original authority relationships remain in the corpus; older documents are never discarded simply because they are older. Both discovery and independent checking still execute. Selection receipts in Inspect distinguish deferred passages from reviewed coverage.

Confirmation uses changed-field source sections, definitions, qualifications, exceptions and dependency closure, including global constraint repairs. Missing source anchors expand the review with an explicit reason. Changed and removed constraint entries are recorded and supplied to confirmation. Original source texts are untouched. Stable transport references eliminate packet-local alias reassignment; existing ambiguous numeric aliases are recorded for rechecking, never guessed or rewritten in historical plans.

Research requires a recorded unmet external factual need and intended use, or an explicit missing industry/market factual item in requirements. Optional background, brand certification and customer permissions do not qualify. The gate precedes reuse of old research; cache keys include the gate version and needs. Skipped research is persisted with zero credits and no provider call. Eligible research retains its unique source bodies unchanged.
